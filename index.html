About LEARN37

Connecting Sri Lanka's Higher Education & Research

LEARN37 is part of the Lanka Education And Research Network (LEARN), bringing together representatives from Sri Lanka's education, research, and technology communities.

This year's programme will place a special focus on cybersecurity in education and research, providing a platform to discuss emerging cyber threats, institutional preparedness, incident response, practical solutions, and collaboration. It also includes the official **LEARN Annual General Meeting (AGM)**.
